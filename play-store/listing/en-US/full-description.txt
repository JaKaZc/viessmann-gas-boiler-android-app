ViGasBoiler shows what your Viessmann gas condensing boiler is doing right now, and lets you change its heating settings safely, straight from your phone.

It uses the official Viessmann API with your own free developer access, so your data goes directly between your phone and Viessmann. No extra account, no cloud service in between.

★ YOUR BOILER AT A GLANCE
• Room temperature against its target, and the outside temperature
• Animated burner indicator with modulation
• Boiler and circuit supply temperatures, system pressure
• Gateway status, Wi-Fi signal and how much of your API budget is left

★ HEATING SETTINGS, CONFIRMED BEFORE SENDING
• Operating mode and room temperature per program (reduced, normal, comfort)
• Weekly schedule on a 24-hour dial: drag the dots to change times, drag a period to move it
• Holiday programs
• Heating curve chart with a live preview while you change slope and shift
• Every change is checked against the boiler's limits and confirmed in a dialog first

★ CHARTS AND STATISTICS
• Room vs target vs outside, supply vs setpoint, modulation and pressure
• Gas use per hour and per day, burner running time and starts
• 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, with tap-to-read values
• Gas, electricity and heat counters with month and year comparison, efficiency
• Export your history as CSV

★ HEATING CHECK
After a week of data, the heating check rates burner cycling, comfort and efficiency against your settings and suggests concrete improvements.

★ HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
• Overview widget with the room card, burner ring and a live countdown to the next refresh
• Statistics widget with twelve key figures
• Both 4×3, so you can stack them

★ MESSAGES AND ALERTS
• Faults and warnings with a banner and notification
• Alerts for low or high pressure, gateway offline and an almost-used-up API budget
• Code lookup in Viessmann's error database

★ MADE TO BE SAFE
• Background refresh every 30 minutes (adjustable), within the API's daily limits, so your ViCare app keeps working
• Settings are never changed in the background, only after your confirmation
• Sign-in tokens encrypted on the device, never backed up
• History and settings return after a reinstall or on a new phone, through Android's backup to your own Google account
• Light and dark theme, or follow your phone
• Optional, opt-in anonymous usage statistics; no ads

WHAT YOU NEED
• A Viessmann boiler connected to the internet (built-in Wi-Fi or Vitoconnect) and set up in the ViCare app
• A free account on the Viessmann developer portal (sign in with your ViCare account)
The setup wizard walks you through creating your API client in a few minutes, with the redirect address ready to copy.

Want to look around first? Tap "Try with sample data" on the first screen: a sample boiler with a week of history, no account needed.

Developed and tested with a Vitodens 100-W on the E3 platform. Screens are built from whatever features your boiler reports, so other E3 boilers should work too; everything the API exposes is reachable under More → All features.
